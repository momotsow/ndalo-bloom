# Ndalo Bloom Social & Sharing Strategy

## Purpose

Social sharing is not an afterthought.

Ndalo Bloom should be designed so that customers naturally want to share the brand.

Primary platforms:

- Instagram
- TikTok
- WhatsApp
- Pinterest

---

# 1. Share the Moment

Customers should share more than a product URL.

They should be able to share:

- Their ritual
- Their gift choice
- Their recommendations
- Their Bloom Moment
- Their unboxing
- Their favourite product

---

# 2. Product Sharing

Product pages should support attractive sharing.

Shared content should include:

- Product image
- Product name
- Brand
- Relevant description
- Open Graph metadata
- Correct preview image

---

# 3. AI Ritual Sharing

Future ritual experiences should produce branded shareable cards.

Example:

> **THE EXHALE**

> Your Sunday evening reset.

with selected products and a Ndalo Bloom visual identity.

---

# 4. Gift Sharing

Gift Finder results can eventually produce shareable recommendations.

For example:

> "I found the perfect birthday gift."

The shared experience should lead back to Ndalo Bloom.

---

# 5. WhatsApp

WhatsApp is especially important for South African social commerce.

Sharing should be:

- Mobile friendly
- Fast
- Easy to understand
- Deep-linked back to the website

---

# 6. Instagram

Support:

- Story sharing
- Product sharing
- Ritual sharing
- Campaign landing pages
- Attractive social previews

---

# 7. TikTok

The website should be designed for traffic originating from TikTok.

Landing pages should immediately communicate:

- What Ndalo Bloom is
- Why it is desirable
- What to do next

---

# 8. Pinterest

Product and editorial pages should have strong imagery and metadata suitable for discovery and sharing.

---

# 9. Bloom Moments

Encourage customer-generated content.

Potential CTA:

> **Show us your Bloom.**

This could eventually feed:

- UGC gallery
- Social proof
- Campaigns
- Community
- Referral programs

---

# 10. Unboxing

The physical package should create a natural social-media moment.

The digital platform can reinforce this through:

- QR codes
- Personalized pages
- Ritual instructions
- Reorder links
- UGC prompts

---

# 11. Attribution

Where technically appropriate, track:

- Social referral
- Campaign
- Share source
- Conversion
- Revenue

This will help determine whether social sharing is producing measurable business value.

---

# 12. Sharing Principle

Do not force social sharing.

The customer should share because:

> "This is beautiful."

> "This is useful."

> "This feels like me."

> "Someone I know needs this."

That is the kind of sharing Ndalo Bloom should create.
