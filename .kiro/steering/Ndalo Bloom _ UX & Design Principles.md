# Ndalo Bloom UX & Design Principles

## 1. Emotional First

The customer should understand the emotional promise before being overwhelmed by products.

The experience should communicate:

> Make time for you.

---

# 2. Luxury Editorial Experience

The storefront should feel closer to a premium beauty/editorial experience than a generic e-commerce template.

Prioritize:

- Typography
- Photography
- Whitespace
- Composition
- Product storytelling
- Sensory language

---

# 3. Feminine Without Being Childish

The design should be feminine but sophisticated.

Avoid:

- Excessive pink
- Cartoon-like illustrations
- Overly cute UI
- Cheap gradients
- Excessive decorative elements

---

# 4. Pink Is an Accent and Brand Signal

Pink should contribute to the identity without becoming the entire interface.

Use supporting neutrals and sophisticated typography to create balance.

---

# 5. Product Photography Matters

Products should feel desirable before the customer reads the description.

Images should support:

- Detail
- Texture
- Scale
- Packaging
- Lifestyle
- Unboxing

---

# 6. Motion With Purpose

Use subtle motion for:

- Transitions
- Discovery
- Product interaction
- AI responses
- Ritual creation

Avoid animation that delays the customer.

---

# 7. Reduce Decision Fatigue

Use:

- Mood
- Occasion
- Gift intent
- Recommendations
- AI
- Curated collections

to help customers choose.

---

# 8. Checkout Is Sacred

The checkout experience should prioritize:

1. Clarity
2. Trust
3. Speed
4. Mobile usability
5. Accessibility

Do not introduce unnecessary animation or discovery features into checkout.

---

# 9. AI Should Not Interrupt

AI should be available when useful.

It should not constantly pop up or cover the shopping experience.

---

# 10. Social Sharing Should Feel Natural

Sharing should happen at moments where customers have something worth sharing:

- Finding a beautiful product
- Creating a ritual
- Choosing a gift
- Receiving an order
- Unboxing

---

# 11. Accessibility

Every interface should support:

- Keyboard users
- Screen readers
- Reduced motion
- Clear focus
- Adequate contrast
- Semantic structure

---

# 12. Mobile First

Design mobile experiences before desktop enhancements.

---

# 13. Fast Feels Premium

Loading states should be:

- Intentional
- Minimal
- Informative

Never make customers stare at blank screens unnecessarily.

---

# 14. Consistency

Buttons, cards, typography, spacing, forms and interactions should behave consistently throughout the application.

---

# 15. Design System

Reusable design primitives should be created rather than repeatedly inventing UI patterns.

The design system should eventually define:

- Typography
- Colours
- Spacing
- Buttons
- Inputs
- Cards
- Modals
- Toasts
- Product components
- Navigation
- AI components
- Checkout components
