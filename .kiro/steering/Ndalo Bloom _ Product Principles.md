# Ndalo Bloom Product Principles

These principles govern product decisions throughout the project.

## 1. Sell the feeling, not only the product

Ndalo Bloom sells moments of self-care.

Every major experience should answer:

> How does this help the customer make time for herself?

---

## 2. Make time for you

The core brand idea is:

> **MAKE TIME FOR YOU.**

The product experience should feel calming, intentional and personal.

---

## 3. Never make the customer feel lost

Customers should feel guided rather than presented with an overwhelming catalogue.

Use:

- Recommendations
- Context
- Mood
- Occasion
- AI
- Product relationships

to simplify discovery.

---

## 4. Luxury means thoughtful

Luxury does not mean adding more visual effects.

Luxury means:

- Thoughtful details
- Excellent typography
- Beautiful imagery
- Smooth interactions
- Clear information
- Fast performance
- Excellent service

---

## 5. Technology should disappear

AI and technology should enhance the experience without making the website feel like a technology demonstration.

---

## 6. Mobile-first

Design for the smallest screen first.

Social traffic is expected to be heavily mobile.

---

## 7. Social-first

Every major product experience should consider whether it can naturally be shared.

Sharing should never feel forced.

---

## 8. Personalization should feel useful

Do not personalize something simply because we can.

Personalization should reduce decision fatigue or increase relevance.

---

## 9. Simplicity beats complexity

Prefer simple systems that solve the actual problem.

Do not introduce:

- Microservices
- Databases
- AI systems
- Third-party services
- Infrastructure

without a clear reason.

---

## 10. Performance is part of luxury

A beautiful website that loads slowly is not a luxury experience.

---

## 11. Accessibility is part of quality

Accessibility must be considered from the beginning.

---

## 12. Customer trust comes first

Never sacrifice:

- Privacy
- Security
- Transparency
- Payment safety
- Accurate product information

for convenience.

---

## 13. AI must be trustworthy

AI may interpret intent.

The application must provide authoritative product and commerce information.

AI must not invent facts.

---

## 14. Build V1 intelligently

The first version should be impressive, reliable and focused.

Do not attempt to build every future idea immediately.

---

## 15. Design for the future without building the future

Architecture should allow future growth without forcing future complexity into V1.

---

## 16. Every feature needs a reason

Before implementing a feature ask:

1. Does it help the customer?
2. Does it support the brand?
3. Does it support the business?
4. Is it worth the complexity?

If the answer is no, reconsider it.
